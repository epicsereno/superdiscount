Ready-to-use prompts for generating brand assets, banners, stickers, and apparel graphics. Print-ready focus: bold shapes, 3–5 color limited palette, high contrast.

**Brand Colors (exact):**
- Red: #F60513
- Gold: #B39E3D / #D8C169
- Black: #0A0A0A / pure black
- White accents
- Optional yellow for vinyl crossover: #FFD100

**Style recommendation:** Piece (full burner) with Blockbuster lettering.  
Why it fits: The real store logo already uses massive squared red DISCOUNT + flowing gold Super. Translates perfectly into a legal-wall burner with depth, forcefield and urban energy while staying print-clean for tees, stickers and site banners. Matches the spray-paint culture the store sells.
